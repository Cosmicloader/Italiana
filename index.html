Our Story

Thirty Years of Hospitality & Craft

Founded in 1995 by the Khanna family, Italiana began as a small family kitchen in the heart of New Delhi. Three decades later, we remain true to our roots — serving the same warmth, atmosphere, and signature flavours across multiple cities.

We believe dining is not merely about food, but about the setting, the service, and the memories created around the table.
