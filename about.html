30 Years of Excellence

Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Grace

Founded in 1995 by the Khanna family, Italiana began as an intimate dining room in New Delhi with one vision — to create an unforgettable restaurant experience rooted in hospitality, atmosphere, and exceptional cuisine.

Over the years, that vision evolved into a signature style: classic European elegance, Indian warmth, handcrafted menus, and service designed to feel personal from the very first visit.

Today, Italiana welcomes guests across multiple cities while preserving the same philosophy that shaped its beginnings — every meal should feel celebratory.

Timeline

The Italiana Journey

From one intimate dining room to a recognized hospitality name across India.

1995 · The Beginning

Italiana opens its first dining room in Connaught Place, New Delhi.

2003 · Signature Menu Era

The restaurant develops its hallmark menu and guest-favourite classics.

2012 · Multi-City Growth

Expansion into Mumbai, Bengaluru, Gurugram, and Chandigarh begins.

2021 · Digital Dining

Reservations, takeaway, and online guest experiences are introduced.

2026 · Today

Italiana continues to grow as a destination for elegant evenings and celebrations.
